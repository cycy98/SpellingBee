Privacy Notice

Spelling Bee is a hobby game. Here is what we collect and why.

What we store

  • Username and password hash — to identify you across sessions. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id and never stored in plain text.
  • Gameplay stats — every guess, WPM, tier, and match result. This is the core of the game; without it there are no leaderboards or profiles.
  • IP address — held in memory for the duration of your session and written to server logs for rate-limiting and abuse prevention. Logs clear automatically.
  • Discord ID — only if you choose to link your account via the Discord bot.

Who can see your data

Your profile and stats are public by default. You can set your profile to private in your account settings — this hides you from the leaderboard and makes your profile page inaccessible to other players.

Your rights

  • Access & export — download all your data as JSON from your account page.
  • Deletion — permanently delete your account and all associated data from your account page. This cannot be undone.

Cookies

We set three cookies: auth (signed login token), session_id (anonymous game session), and local_sessions (pass-and-play mode). All are strictly necessary for the game to function.

Data retention

Data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes everything immediately.

Contact

Questions? Go on Discord to reach out to the admin.
